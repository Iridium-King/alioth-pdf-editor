Tesseract.js Core 5.0.0 and Tesseract trained data

The WASM files in this directory are browser builds of the Tesseract OCR engine
from tesseract.js-core. The Spanish and English models are the 4.0.0_best_int
trained data distributed by the Tesseract.js project.

The engine and the Tesseract models are licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0, reproduced in LICENSE.

Upstream projects:
https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
https://github.com/naptha/tessdata

origins.json records the exact package versions and SHA-256 checksums.
